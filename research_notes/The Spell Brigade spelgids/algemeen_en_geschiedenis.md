# The Spell Brigade: general facts, history and reception (state as of 2026-10-12)

## 1. Who made it: developer, publisher, location, other games

### Takeaway
Bolt Blaster Games developed and self-published The Spell Brigade. It is a small Belgian (Flemish) indie studio, usually described as six people and reportedly based in Diepenbeek, Limburg. Its only earlier game is the VR roguelite shooter GAZZLERS (2023). External PR is handled by Pirate PR.

### Cited Findings
- The developer is Bolt Blaster Games, described as "a Belgian indie studio" — [CogConnected (Apr 2024)](https://cogconnected.com/2024/04/the-spell-brigade-will-cast-its-magic-on-steam-later-this-year/); [AnimationXpress](https://www.animationxpress.com/latest-news/belgiums-bolt-blaster-games-announces-launch-date-of-its-latest-title-the-spell-brigade/)

## 4. Genre, self-description, engine, player count, co-op, languages

### Takeaway
The developers present it as a top-down **online co-op survivors-like / "bullet heaven" roguelite for 1–4 players**. Its signature hooks are friendly fire and spell synergies, and it is openly Magicka-inspired. Solo play is supported. Crossplay between Steam and PS5 arrived with 1.0. The store lists English plus 16 other languages, and the game is Steam Deck Verified. I could not confirm the engine.

### Cited Findings
- Store-page style self-description (from search snippets, not a verbatim fetch): "The Spell Brigade is an online co-op Survivors-like for 1-4 players"; players "complete team-based objectives, unlock new upgrades and create overpowered spell synergies"; "You can use your deaths to your advantage, and use Revive Cores to (literally) blast yourself and your teammates back into action!" — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2904000/The_Spell_Brigade); [GamesPress PR](https://gamespress.com/Gather-round-Wizards-The-Spell-Brigade-a-Magicka-inspired-1-4-player-s)

Early Access ran from v0.6 (Sep 2024) to v0.13 (late 2025 / early 2026). Major updates were named and numbered "EA Update 8–13", each adding a wizard and/or a realm, boss or element. 1.0 came on 29 April 2026, followed by numbered 1.0 patches (Patch 9, around 1 July 2026, brought a post-1.0 roadmap). **Update 1.1 "Realm Sweep" (2 Sep 2026)** added wizard Eugene/Eugenie, and **1.2 (6 Oct 2026)** is the latest version found. The developers say a "bigger update" is coming, planned for around the end of 2026. The game is **not** in Early Access as of 12 Oct 2026.

## 6. Reception

### Takeaway
Players rate it **"Very Positive" on Steam** (about 87–90% positive; 13,000+ reviews by March 2026). It sold **over one million copies during Early Access** and peaked at about **14,000–14,500 concurrent Steam players around April 2025**. Critics at 1.0 were mixed to positive, ranging from about 5/10 to 7/10. They praised the co-op, friendly-fire chaos and spell synergies, and criticised the grindy meta-progression and limited variety. I found no Metacritic score and no awards.
